What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a screening measurement calculated using a person's height and weight.

BMI Categories

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal range
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 and above Obesity range

BMI is a general screening measurement and does not directly measure body fat or muscle mass. Individual health circumstances can vary.
