Indian Foods Rich in Protein

Include a variety of protein-rich foods in your normal meals. Values below are approximate and vary by preparation.

  • 1 large egg: about 6–7 g protein
  • 250 ml milk: about 8 g protein
  • 100 g cooked chicken: about 25–31 g protein
  • 100 g cooked fish: about 20–26 g protein
  • 100 g cooked dal: about 6–9 g protein
  • 100 g tofu: about 8–17 g protein, depending on type
  • 30 g dry soya chunks: often about 15–16 g protein
  • 100 g curd: about 3–4 g protein

Food weights matter: dry soya chunks and cooked soya chunks have different protein concentrations per 100 g.

How is protein calculated?

Daily protein estimate = body weight in kilograms multiplied by the selected protein factor in grams per kilogram per day.

For example, 70 kg × 1.2 g/kg = 84 g protein per day. Your actual needs can vary with age, health, body composition, training and overall diet.
