Protein in Common Indian Foods

Approximate protein values per typical serving. Actual values depend on preparation and portion size.

1 large egg 6–7 g protein
Milk (250 ml) About 8 g protein
Cooked chicken (100 g) 25–31 g protein
Cooked fish (100 g) 20–26 g protein
Cooked dal (1 cup) 6–9 g protein
Soya chunks (30 g dry) 15–16 g protein
Tofu (100 g) About 8–17 g protein
Curd (100 g) About 3–4 g protein
Tip: Eggs, dal, milk, curd, fish and soya chunks can help you meet your daily protein target without relying entirely on expensive supplements.

Protein Needs by Goal

These are broad estimates for generally healthy adults, not exact requirements for every person.

Sedentary / Low Activity 0.8–1.0 g per kg of body weight
General Fitness / Maintenance 1.0–1.2 g per kg of body weight
Active Lifestyle / Sports 1.2–1.6 g per kg of body weight
Muscle Gain / Bodybuilding 1.4–2.0 g per kg of body weight
Fat Loss / Preserve Muscle 1.4–2.0 g per kg of body weight
If you have kidney disease or another condition that affects protein intake, ask a healthcare professional for an appropriate target.
