What are maintenance calories?

Maintenance calories are an estimate of the number of calories your body needs each day to maintain its current weight.

What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It is an estimate of how much energy your body uses while at rest.

How is TDEE calculated?

This calculator estimates BMR using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to estimate TDEE.
